Our Structure

Two teams, one mission

🏛 Core Team

The Core Team provides governance, oversight, and final approval. They set the direction of the initiative, coordinate with the School Committee, and ensure the process runs smoothly.

Members: Andrew · Liz · Jen

⚙️ Review Team

The Review Team is made up of representatives from every school community. They expand, refine, and evaluate submitted ideas — drawing on their direct experience with each activity.

Representing: Each AB school PTO, SEPAC, Cultural Groups, Teachers, and Administrators

Community Forums

Monthly open meetings

Once a month, Common Thread hosts an open forum where community members gather to discuss their favorite things from elementary school. These are casual, welcoming events — no prior experience needed.

What to expect at a forum

  • A brief recap of recently published activities
  • Open discussion — what traditions matter to your family?
  • QR codes and clipboards with the quick submission form
  • Refreshments and informal connection time
  • All community members welcome — no RSVP required
View upcoming dates →

The Review Process

How ideas get expanded

  1. An idea is submitted through the quick form — either online or at a community forum.
  2. The Core Team assigns the idea to the relevant Review Team group based on topic and expertise.
  3. Review Team members with direct experience activating the event expand the entry — adding logistics, costs, contacts, and step-by-step guidance.
  4. The Review Team may send the idea back for more community input, add detail themselves, or promote it to the Steering Committee for approval.
  5. Once approved, the activity is published to the catalog with a full playbook and PDF download — available to every AB school.
  6. At each monthly forum, the latest published activities are shared and discussed with the community.

Who we are, what we're building, and how to get involved.

Our Purpose

Why Common Thread?

The Common Thread Task Force supports community connection and shared culture across Acton-Boxborough by identifying, organizing, and activating enrichment and engagement opportunities for elementary school families.

Acton-Boxborough already has a long history of successful enrichment and community activities. These efforts often emerge from individual schools, PTO groups, teachers, or volunteers, but they are rarely documented or shared district-wide. This initiative aims to preserve that legacy and make it accessible to everyone.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can submit an idea?

Anyone connected to the Acton-Boxborough community — parents, caregivers, teachers, staff, or community members. No Google account required.

What kinds of ideas can I submit?

Any enrichment or engagement activity that has been run at or with AB schools — clubs, events, field trips, family programs, summer gatherings, and more.

How long does review take?

The Review Team aims to process submissions within two weeks. You'll receive an email update when your idea moves through the process.

Can I add more detail after I submit?

Yes. Your confirmation email includes a link to expand your submission at any time before it's approved.

What happens when an idea is approved?

Approved activities are added to the Activity Catalog with a full playbook — a step-by-step guide and downloadable PDF available to every AB school.

Is this connected to the School Committee?

The Common Thread Task Force operates under the Acton-Boxborough Regional School District with School Committee sponsorship.

How long will the site be available?

The website will remain available as long as it continues to serve the community. There is no planned end date.
